## What I build

- **Computer architecture and compilers:** [Tiny Computers](https://github.com/tinycomputers) is a series of small, readable systems you can watch run: [TinyGPU](https://github.com/tinycomputers/tiny-gpu) makes SIMT execution visible in Python; [TinyTPU](https://github.com/tinycomputers/tiny-tpu), [TinyCPU](https://github.com/tinycomputers/tiny-cpu), and [TinyAttention](https://github.com/tinycomputers/tiny-attention) run SystemVerilog designs in the browser through WebAssembly; [TinyCompiler](https://github.com/tinycomputers/tiny-compiler) targets the GPU and TPU from one tensor program.
- **Machine learning and data engineering:** the [Telco Churn MLOps Pipeline](https://github.com/deaneeth/telco-churn-mlops-pipeline) combines MLflow, Spark, Kafka, Airflow, and Dockerized inference; the [E-commerce Repeat Buyer Prediction](https://github.com/deaneeth/ecommerce-repeat-buyer-prediction) pipeline processes 20.7M clickstream events with PySpark, Databricks, and Spark MLlib (AUC-ROC 0.917).
- **Edge and applied AI:** the [Smart Beehive Monitoring System](https://github.com/deaneeth/smart-beehive-monitor) connects solar-powered ESP32 sensing to Firebase and a [React/TypeScript dashboard](https://github.com/deaneeth/smart-beehive-dashboard); the [Multi-Omics Cancer Subtype Classifier](https://github.com/deaneeth/multi-omics-cancer-subtype-classifier) applies XGBoost, PyTorch, biological pathways, SHAP, and integrated gradients to cancer subtype classification.
